How to Play Cubiku

Cubiku is a 3-dimensional Sudoku puzzle. Instead of a flat 9x9 grid, you are solving a 3x3x3 cube, which has been sliced into three flat boards (z=0, z=1, and z=2).

The Goal

Fill every empty cell with a number from 1 to 9.

The 3 Rules of the Cube

To win, every number (1-9) must appear exactly once in every 3x3 cross-section of the cube:

  • 1. The Visible Slices (Z-Planes): Each of the three 3x3 boards on your screen must contain the numbers 1-9 exactly once.
  • 2. The Rows (Y-Planes): If you take Row 1 from z=0, Row 1 from z=1, and Row 1 from z=2, those 9 cells form a flat plane cutting through the cube. They must contain 1-9 exactly once.
  • 3. The Columns (X-Planes): Similarly, Column 1 from z=0, Column 1 from z=1, and Column 1 from z=2 form a plane. They must contain 1-9 exactly once.
Pro Tip: Tap a clue number on the board to instantly highlight every instance of it. You can tap a keypad number first and then cells, or tap a cell first and then the keypad!
