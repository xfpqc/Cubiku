Welcome to Cubiku!

Solve a 3×3×3 cube sliced into three flat boards (z = 0, 1, 2). Place digits 1–9 with no repeats in:

  • Each 3×3 Slice: Every flat board contains 1–9 once.
  • Horizontal Rows: Every row spanning across all three boards contains 1–9 once.
  • Vertical Columns: Every column spanning across all three boards contains 1–9 once.
